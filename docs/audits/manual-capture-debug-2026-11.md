Motion energy is the mean 3D distance of finite paired points from consecutive analyzed frames: pose indices 11–16 and paired hand landmarks. The mean is divided by 0.05, clamped to 1 and rounded; it is per analyzed frame, not velocity per second. In automatic waiting, 0.12 is the dynamic start threshold (two qualifying frames, with decay on lower energy). During automatic capture, 0.045 is the end stability threshold, held 420 ms plus three post-roll frames while usable tracking continues. The 0.035 threshold applies to rest and cooldown. If either anchor y is at or above shoulder mean y plus max(0.12, 0.9 × shoulder width), it is in the signing zone.
