# Physical camera diagnostics — 2026-11-02

The physical webcam was not available for this investigation. The cause of missing fingers and the cause of the observed maximum-duration attempts remain unconfirmed. The bundle served before this change contained the hand visibility exception; a pre-fix frontend was therefore not the explanation at that time. The detector copies MediaPipe's left and right hand arrays, and the overlay clears and redraws each analyzed frame with the same centered cover crop and mirror as the video. Pose wrists are drawn separately. A successful canvas draw count is a count of drawing calls, not proof that points are within the visible crop or perceptible to the user.

The local diagnostic panel now shows per-hand raw, finite x/y, and drawn counts, dimensions, mirror state and frame/draw timestamps. Measurements are local and transient. No physical-camera values were collected. Motion diagnostics expose the current energy, threshold, usable-frame flag, zone, stable duration, and actual segmenter events. The timestamp is the animation-frame monotonic timestamp in milliseconds, not wall time.

Segmentation waits for dynamic motion (energy 0.12 for two frames) or a stable static signing pose (750 ms), then captures until energy is at most 0.045 for 420 ms plus three post-roll frames. Minimum duration is 500 ms and at least eight usable frames are required. At 6000 ms the segment is rejected before inference. A prior defect allowed an interruption with no usable hand/shoulder frame to accumulate end stability; that is now reset. This defect does not establish the cause of the user's timeout. Noise or continual motion above the threshold could prevent stability; real measurements are needed. Cooldown requires 800 ms and 350 ms of low-energy rest as defined by absent hands or the learned baseline. Rest detection can also require live evaluation.

For attempt accounting, captured/analyzed is the count of detector frames in the segment, including pre-roll, rather than camera hardware exposures. Hand and pose coverage divide their respective counts by that same segment-frame count. A usable frame requires a detected/finite hand, detected pose and finite shoulders; accepted and rejected counts partition the segment, with non-overlapping reasons. `processed_frames` is retained for compatibility: on rejection it is usable frames, while after payload construction it is the sequence builder's valid source-frame count; `sequence_length` is the resampled payload length. The counters are not detector FPS or proof of a reliable boundary. The segmentation diagnostics preserve the existing rejection of incomplete segments and no model thresholds were changed.
